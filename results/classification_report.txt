Evaluation Metrics

• Accuracy

Measures the overall percentage of correctly classified images.

---------------------------------------------------------

• Precision

Indicates how many predicted samples of a class actually belong to that class.

---------------------------------------------------------

• Recall

Measures how many actual samples of a class are correctly identified.

---------------------------------------------------------

• F1 Score

Harmonic mean of Precision and Recall.

---------------------------------------------------------

• Confusion Matrix

Shows the number of correct and incorrect predictions for each class.
